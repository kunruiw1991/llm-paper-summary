2. Closed-Form Rank-One Update (ROME) vs. MEMIT

Instead of noisy gradient descent, ROME solves a constrained least-squares objective directly:

ΔW = (v_* - W k_*) (C^{-1} k_*)^T / (k_*^T C^{-1} k_*) Key Properties: - Exact recall: (W + ΔW) k_* = v_* - Minimal distortion: preserves pretraining covariance C = E[k k^T] - Locality: retention of unrelated facts remains >92%
