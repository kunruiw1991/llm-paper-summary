1. The GPU Memory Hierarchy & Bandwidth Gap

Standard attention is severely bottlenecked by the 12× bandwidth gap between On-Chip SRAM (Shared Memory) and Off-Chip HBM (DRAM).

SRAM (On-Chip Shared Memory) ~19.0 TB/s (192 KB / SM)

Ultra-fast cache, but extremely small capacity.

HBM2 (High Bandwidth Memory) 1.555 TB/s (40-80 GB)

Large capacity, but 12.2× slower bandwidth!

Standard Attention HBM Traffic: Θ(N·d + N²)
FlashAttention HBM Traffic: Θ(N²·d² / M)
When M ≥ 4d, FlashAttention reduces HBM traffic by a factor of Θ(M / d).
