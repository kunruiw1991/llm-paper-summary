2. Coconut: Chain of Continuous Thought

Discrete autoregressive LLMs force intermediate reasoning steps into discrete tokens (argmax or sampling), prematurely collapsing probability distributions onto a single greedy branch. Coconut feeds hidden state h_t directly back as continuous input:
