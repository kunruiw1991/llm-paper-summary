Foundation 1

Narrative-to-Tilemap Grounding (Word2World)

Children love crafting stories with bunnies, rivers, enchanted caves, and golden carrots. Word2World parses natural language story tokens into formal spatial entities:

  • Protagonist & Spawn: Bella the Bunny → Start position S
  • Goal Objective: Golden Carrot on Hill → Goal position G
  • Biome Terrain: Rushing Blue River → Water tiles ~
  • Obstacles & Barriers: Sleeping Hedgehogs / Stones → Walls #
  • Pacing & Rewards: Dewdrops & Fruit → Collectible Stars *

Instead of hand-crafting levels, the LLM maps story beats into a 2D matrix of discrete affordances.

The Core Obstacle

Why Raw LLMs Fail Global 2D Topology

Autoregressive language models predict tokens sequentially in raster order (row-by-row, left-to-right). Because autoregressive attention lacks explicit topological graph constraints:

  • The Severed River: An LLM generates a river all the way from row 0 to 7, but forgets to insert a bridge, completely partitioning the world.
  • Trapped Spawn: Clustered wall tokens enclose the player in an unreachable 1x1 pocket.
  • Empirical Solvability: Raw LLMs achieve only 60.0% solvability out of the box, frustrating young players.
The Word2World Innovation

The Iterative A* Solvability Repair Loop

Word2World fixes spatial hallucinations with a closed-loop verification cycle:

  1. A* Graph Search: Runs exact pathfinding from S to G using the admissible Manhattan distance heuristic.
  2. Frontier Cut Detection: If no path exists, the solver identifies the critical obstacle tiles along the search frontier that are closest to the goal.
  3. Surgical Repair: Replaces blocking water ~ with bridges = or clears brambles # to open floor ..
  4. Convergence: Guarantees 100% solvability in ≤ 3 iterations while preserving 95%+ of the original story layout!
Child Experience

Pacing, Winding Factor & Safe Sandbox

A great child game level must be neither an empty corridor nor an exhausting maze:

  • Winding Factor λ: Ratio of path length to Manhattan distance d(S, G). Word2World targets λ ∈ [1.10, 1.45] for playful exploration.
  • Reward Placement: Collectible stars are placed on reachable branch cells, reinforcing curiosity without blocking completion.
  • Child Safety Firewall: All story prompts and token vocabularies are strictly verified against harmful content.
